Key Questions to Ask Doctors

Use these in conversations with healthcare providers:

  • • "What specific medical goals must be met before discharge?"
  • • "What are the risks of discharging on this timeline?"
  • • "How will we monitor for complications after discharge?"
  • • "Is there a clinical reason we can't delay discharge 24-48 hours?"
  • • "What home health services are being arranged?"
  • • "Who do I call if a complication occurs?"
